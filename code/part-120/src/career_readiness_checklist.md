# Final Career-Readiness Checklist

## Technical
- I define result grain before writing SQL.
- I state NULL, duplicate, tie, time-zone, and boundary rules.
- I write deterministic queries and predict expected rows.
- I defend keys, constraints, normalization, and history choices.
- I protect invariants under races, retries, and ambiguous outcomes.
- I read execution plans with actual evidence.
- I explain compatible migrations, security, reconciliation, and restore.

## Interview execution
- I clarify before coding.
- I narrate in concise stages without filling silence with guesses.
- I test a small adversarial case.
- I admit uncertainty and propose how to verify it.
- I finish with trade-offs, risks, and review triggers.

## Portfolio
- Two projects are reproducible from a clean environment.
- Every major claim links to SQL, tests, plans, metrics, or runbooks.
- README files explain problem, architecture, invariants, setup, evidence, and limitations.
- Secrets and personal data are absent.
- Demo paths are short and reliable.

## Career launch
- Resume bullets are truthful and evidence-backed.
- Role-specific stories are prepared at 30-second, 3-minute, and 10-minute depth.
- A weekly application and revision cadence is scheduled.
- Rejections and weak mocks become input to the error log, not proof of inability.
